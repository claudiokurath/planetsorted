    How it works

    The Autopilot Engine

    Four simple rules turn your raw numbers into a personalized money system.

    01

    Savings Rate

    We calculate MAX(0, (Income − Expenses) / Income) to find exactly how much of every paycheck is truly free to work for you.

    02

    Investment Allocation

    Your risk tolerance sets the split: below 3 routes 30% of your surplus to investing, 3 and above routes 70% — the rest accelerates savings & debt payoff.

    03

    Retirement Contribution

    Less than 10 years to retirement? We recommend 20% of income. 10+ years away? A steadier 10% keeps you on track without straining your budget.

    04

    Automation

    With Automate Investments switched on, your allocation is treated as a standing monthly transfer — no manual decisions, no missed months.

    FAQ

    Common Questions

    Is my financial data stored anywhere?

    No. Financial Autopilot runs entirely in your browser. Your numbers are used only to generate your on-screen plan and are never sent to a server or saved.

    How is my Debt-to-Income ratio calculated?

    We divide your total debt by your annualized income (monthly income × 12) to show how your debt load compares to what you earn in a year.

    What does "risk tolerance" change?

    It changes how aggressively your monthly surplus is allocated to investing versus savings and debt paydown — higher risk tolerance leans into long-term growth.

    Is this financial advice?

    No. Financial Autopilot is an educational planning tool that illustrates general strategies. It does not constitute personalized financial, tax, or investment advice — consult a licensed professional for decisions specific to your situation.
