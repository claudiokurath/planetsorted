Working Process

From heartbeat to healthier habit

A lightweight middleware layer sits between your wallet and your wearable, checking your stress state before every transaction.

01

Connect your wearable

A brief onboarding grants access to your wearable's heart‑rate data via HealthKit or Google Fit — no extra hardware needed.

02

Continuous stress monitoring

The system watches physiological stress markers in the background, especially in the moments around a payment attempt.

03

The middleware checks in

When a transaction is initiated, the middleware reads the latest heart‑rate value against your personal threshold (default 110 bpm).

04

Pause & breathe

If stress is high, the payment is auto‑paused and a one‑minute breathing exercise kicks in before you can re‑authorize.

05

Confirm, mindfully

After the cooldown, you decide whether to complete the purchase — turning impulse into intention.

Usage Instructions

Get started in six simple steps

No new hardware, no complicated setup — just connect the health data you already track.

  1. 1Install the Friction Wallet app on your iOS or Android device.
  2. 2Connect the app to your HealthKit (iOS) or Google Fit (Android) account.
  3. 3Set your personal stress threshold (default is 110 bpm).
  4. 4Make a payment as usual; the app automatically checks your heart‑rate.
  5. 5If a stress spike is detected, follow the one‑minute breathing prompt, then re‑authorize.
  6. 6Review weekly summaries in the app to track progress and adjust thresholds.

Under the Hood

A simple middleware, a powerful pause

A middleware layer checks physiological stress markers before authorizing NFC/digital payments — built natively for both major mobile ecosystems.

Swift (iOS) Kotlin (Android) HealthKit API Google Fit API
logic_blueprint.py 
def authorize_transaction(request):
    current_hr = health_api.get_latest_heart_rate()
    if current_hr > 110:  # Threshold for 'Stress/Impulse' state
        trigger_cooldown_lock(duration=60)
        return "Transaction Paused: Complete 1-minute breathing to unlock."
    return "Authorized"
