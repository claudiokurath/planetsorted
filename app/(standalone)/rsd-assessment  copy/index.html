What is RSD?

Rejection Sensitive Dysphoria (RSD) describes an intense emotional response to perceived criticism, rejection, or failure — often making a neutral or ambiguous message feel like a personal attack. RSD Reality Check doesn't diagnose or replace professional support; it's a quick, structured pause that helps you separate the facts of a message from the emotional story your mind may be adding, so you can respond thoughtfully instead of reactively.

Managers & Team Leaders

Handle ambiguous feedback and delicate conversations with composure.

Sales Professionals

Navigate negotiations and client messages without over-reacting.

Remote Workers, Educators & CS Agents

Respond thoughtfully to real-time written communication all day long.
